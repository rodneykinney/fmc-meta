Use it to refine your FMC strategy and practice case recognition

Start with scramble("...") to load a scramble or just scramble to generate a new scramble

Solving Individual Steps

Move from step to step by typing the name of the step you want to work on. The display will show the bad corners/edges for the cube in its current state. Available steps are: eofb,eorl,eoud,drud,drrl,drfb,htr,fb

When starting a step, the cube will reset its orientation to put the DR axis on top and the EO axis in front. You can change the orientation by entering x, y', z2, etc. You can also fine-tune the orientation by dragging the mouse in the display.

Enter moves (R, F', U2, etc.) to apply those moves to the cube. Enter niss to switch between normal and inverse scrambles. Enter reset to return to the beginning of the current step.

You can enter solve at any point to find the shortest unknown solution to the current step. Enter solve(n) to find the next n solutions (up to 50 at at time)

Building a Full Solution

When the step is solved, enter save to save to the list of solutions at the bottom. If the step is not solved, you can still save it. Enter back to return to the previous step.

Enter comment("...") to specify a comment for the current step To indicate you are through checking a step, enter cross_out to draw a line through it

Click on any step at the bottom to see the steps and precede or follow it. Double click or enter check("name", #) to load a step and start working on it. The steps that are active in the display are drawn in bold

Saving/Loading Your Session

Enter save_session("...") to save all of your activity so far to a log file. You can load a logfile by entering load_session("...")
